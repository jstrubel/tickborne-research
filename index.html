Lyme Randomized Controlled Trial Dec 28, 2025

Extended doxycycline therapy shows 23% improvement in post-treatment Lyme disease syndrome symptoms at 6-month follow-up

✨ AI Plain-English Summary

What they found: Patients with lingering Lyme symptoms who took an additional 8 weeks of doxycycline showed meaningful improvement compared to placebo. The key insight: Those who started treatment within 12 months of their initial diagnosis saw the best results, suggesting earlier intervention matters. Limitations: The study couldn't determine whether benefits persist after stopping antibiotics.

Bartonella Research Article Dec 26, 2025

Novel PCR diagnostic identifies Bartonella in 34% of patients previously diagnosed with seronegative rheumatoid arthritis

✨ AI Plain-English Summary

What they found: Using a more sensitive DNA test, researchers discovered that 1 in 3 patients diagnosed with a specific type of rheumatoid arthritis actually had Bartonella infection. Why it matters: 72% of these misdiagnosed patients improved with antibiotic treatment — suggesting many autoimmune diagnoses may actually be treatable infections. For patients: If you have seronegative RA that isn't responding to standard treatment, Bartonella testing may be worth discussing with your doctor.

🔬 34% tested positive
💊 72% improved with antibiotics
Breitschwerdt EB, et al. · Clinical Infectious Diseases
